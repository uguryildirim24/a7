# Bupropion, its metabolites and alpha7: evidence map

Public edition: October 8, 2026. This is a focused critical narrative review,
not a registered systematic review or meta-analysis.

The [literature table](bupropion_receptor_evidence.csv) retains 104 endpoint-specific
observations with 84 distinct PMID identifiers. Each row separates compound,
stereochemistry, receptor, preparation, method, finding, source verification,
source location and limitation. Rows include comparator and downstream studies.
They are not 104 independent confirmations of either proposed mechanism.

The authoritative [Markdown manuscript](bupropion_alpha7_hypothesis_draft.md)
formulates two hypotheses, with separate predictions and rejection criteria.
The [review record](docs/review_record.md) explains corrected and unresolved claims.

## Direct receptor evidence

Parent bupropion has experimentally measured alpha7 inhibition. Reported potency
belongs to its preparation, agonist, exposure time and endpoint. It is not a
measurement of target engagement in a person receiving treatment.

| Study | Observation retained in the literature table | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Slemmer 2000, PMID 10991997 | Functional nicotinic subtype inhibition | Subtype rankings are assay-specific |
| Alkondon 2005, PMID 15647329 | No inhibition of the alpha7-like response at 1 micromolar | A native-slice response class is not every alpha7 preparation |
| Papke 2011, PMID 21285282 | Alpha7 IC50 46 +/- 7 micromolar during coapplication and 16 +/- 3 after bath exposure | Agonist concentrations and timing also differed |
| Vazquez-Gomez 2014, PMID 25016090 | Calcium-response IC50 54 micromolar and radioligand competition estimate Ki 63 micromolar | Does not locate a metabolite in human alpha7 |
| Duarte 2021, PMID 33668529 | Rat alpha7-containing current ratio 0.52 +/- 0.04 at 50 micromolar | A response ratio is not a fitted IC50 |

Lukas 2010, PMID 20509659, tested stereoselective hydroxybupropion activity at
alpha4beta2 and alpha3beta4*, among other non-alpha7 receptors. Its panel did not
include alpha7. Its values must not be relabelled as alpha7 potency. Damaj 2004 and
Bondarev 2003 remain limited by incomplete original full-text access in this review.

For hydroxy-, threo-, erythro-, minor and conjugated metabolites, absence of a
verified alpha7 result in this review means an evidence gap. It does not mean
inactive, never studied or absent from all literature. Urinary structural
identification is not evidence of brain exposure or receptor function.

## Circuit and clinical links

Alpha7 can recruit inhibition in specific circuits. Other preparations show
opposite or null effects. The manuscript retains those constraints:

- The injury/amygdala study reported increased alpha7 activity alongside reduced
  GABA inhibition, not alpha7 loss.
- The septum/hippocampus seizure experiment identified somatostatin-positive cells,
  rather than PV-positive cells, as necessary downstream participants.
- A genetic sensory-gating study did not establish the proposed P50/PPI association.
- Dale's small depersonalization comparison group showed a PPI pattern resembling
  controls. This is not an equivalence demonstration.
- The bupropion/paroxetine trial reported no differential effect on a broad
  Disturbed Thinking composite. It did not report an isolated DP/DR treatment effect.
- Rat brain-distribution measurements and predicted human concentrations do not
  establish human alpha7 occupancy.
- A retracted 2019 alpha7/seizure article was excluded. The distinct 2021 Sun article
  was not treated as replication of it.

The proposed drug/receptor/circuit/symptom chain remains untested. Alternative
nicotinic subtypes, 5-HT3 effects, named potassium channels and non-receptor
explanations remain relevant. No pooled potency or exposure model is supplied.

## Docking contribution

The [parent report](parent_docking_findings.md) and
[combined report](combined_metabolite_docking_report.md) describe candidate
geometries, not measured binding. Site assignment is sensitive to setup. Both
8V82 and 8V8A are ligand-bound intermediate templates, not an activated/desensitized
pair. The failed top-pose control and interrupted supplement remain disclosed.

## Review coverage and gaps

Consensus supported discovery. Scite supported citation tracing and access to
indexed text. PubMed, primary articles and prescribing information supplied
verification. Primary text controlled values when snippets or OCR disagreed.
Automated citation labels were not treated as replication.

A complete screening log and the full discovery pool are not in this tree.
Some dated queries and counts remain in the historical research records. They do
not provide a reproducible systematic review. A search negative is not a novelty claim.

Remaining work includes full-text access to Damaj, Bondarev and Vazquez-Gomez,
bupropion-specific supplementary contact comparison, and faculty review of the
hypotheses, exposure assumptions and source interpretation. No new docking,
exposure model or circuit simulation was performed for this evidence map.
